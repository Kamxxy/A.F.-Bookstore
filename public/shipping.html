SHIPPING & DELIVERY

How your books arrive.

Everything you need to know about how we handle, deliver and track your orders.

What happens after you order

  • Your order is placed and a stock reservation is made for your books.
  • Once payment is confirmed, your order moves to processing.
  • We then prepare your books for delivery, and you can follow the status on your tracking page.

Delivery timing

Delivery time varies by location and is confirmed at checkout when your order is placed. Every order passes through the same stages — Pending, Processing, Shipped and Delivered — so timing can vary between cities.

Tracking your order

Use your order ID on the Track Order page at any time to see the latest payment and delivery status.

If your order is delayed or incorrect

If your order is taking longer than expected, appears to be incorrect, or arrives damaged, please contact us through the support channel on the Contact page with your order ID so we can investigate.
