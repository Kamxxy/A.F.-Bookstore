PRIVACY

Your privacy.

A plain-language summary of the information this website collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Customer name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Delivery address, city and state
  • Order information (books, quantities, totals)
  • Payment information handled through Paystack (for example transaction reference, amount, currency and payment method)

What we do not store

We do not store card numbers, PINs or online banking credentials. Card details are entered directly on Paystack's hosted checkout page and are processed by Paystack under their own privacy policy.

Why we collect it

  • To create and fulfil your order
  • To process and verify your payment
  • To deliver your order to the correct address
  • To track and manage your order
  • To respond to support enquiries

Third-party services

  • Paystack — payment processing and verification
  • Cloudinary — book cover image hosting
  • MongoDB (MongoDB Atlas when hosted) — order and catalogue storage

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information associated with your order details by contacting us via the Contact page with your order ID.
