The simple rules for using this website and buying from A.F. Bookstore.

Using the website

Content on this site is provided for browsing and purchasing books. Do not misuse the site, attempt to disrupt it, or misrepresent your identity when placing an order.

Products, prices & stock

Book details, cover images and prices are shown to the best of our ability. Stock is reserved for a limited payment window after an order is placed. Availability can change between browsing and checkout.

Orders & payment

  • An order is recorded when checkout completes, with status pending.
  • Payment is processed securely through Paystack, and must be completed within the reservation window.
  • If payment is not confirmed in time, the stock reservation expires and the order is cancelled.
  • We reserve the right to cancel or refuse orders in cases of unavailability, pricing error, suspected fraud or incomplete payment.
