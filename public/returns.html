RETURNS & REFUNDS

Something not right?

If a book arrives damaged, is missing, or is not the one you ordered, we want to make it right.

When to contact us

  • The book you receive is damaged or defective.
  • You received the wrong book.
  • Part of your order is missing.
  • Your order status or payment status looks wrong.

How to report a problem

Reach us through the support channel on the Contact page and include your order ID, a short description of the problem, and a photo of the item if it arrived damaged.

Refunds at a glance

Where a refund is due, it is issued back to the original payment method used on Paystack. Exact eligibility always depends on the situation and our final policy.

Note: because payment confirmation is handled automatically, payment status and refunds are linked — an order that was never paid cannot be refunded, and a payment that was verified as successful is recorded on the order.

Our policy

Formal eligibility windows and refund timelines are set by the bookstore and communicated at the time a return is approved. Nothing on this page limits any statutory rights you may have.
