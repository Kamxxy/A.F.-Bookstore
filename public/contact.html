CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Contact us.

We are readers too, and we are happy to help you with your order or anything else about the store.

What we can help with

  • Orders — checking, changing or confirming an order
  • Payments — payment status, failed payments, receipts
  • Delivery — tracking, delays, damaged or incorrect deliveries
  • Returns & refunds
  • General bookstore enquiries and recommendations

How to reach us

Support channel

A direct contact channel (email, phone or WhatsApp) will be listed here once the bookstore publishes its official details. Until then, please track your order online at Track Order and use your order ID when making any enquiry.

Tip: always include your order ID (for example AFORDER-1787836554267-7974) so we can help you faster.

Before you contact us

Most answers are already available on our help pages: Shipping & Delivery, Returns & Refunds, and order tracking.
